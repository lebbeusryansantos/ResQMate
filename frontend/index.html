Frequently Asked Questions

ResQMate is designed for community users to request emergency assistance (Food, Water, Shelter, Medicine, General), and for authorized staff and administrators to review, manage, and dispatch relief distributions.

Once logged in, you can navigate to the 'My Requests' dashboard to view real-time updates and status changes on any active requests you have submitted.

ResQMate is a supplemental coordination tool. For immediate life-threatening emergencies, please contact your local hotline or emergency services (like 911 or local LGU rescue units) right away.
